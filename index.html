THE FIELD GUIDE

Find your way.

Orbit
Drag to rotate. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag or use two fingers to pan.
Plan
Look down into the pavilions. Drag to pan and scroll to zoom.
Walk
Use WASD or arrow keys to move. Drag the view to look around, or click to capture the mouse. Escape releases it. On touch screens, drag the scene to look and hold the thumbstick in the direction you want to move.
Doors and lights
Use the sidebar to control each door and bedroom independently. In Walk mode, press E near a door or L near a bedroom switch. You can also click a nearby door or wall switch.
